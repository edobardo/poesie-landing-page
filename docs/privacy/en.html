Privacy Policy

How we process your data in Poesie

We do not collect or store the contents of your poems on our servers: your collection always remains under your control on your device, in your Google Drive, or on Microsoft OneDrive.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Introduction

Poesie is an Android mobile application that allows users to create, manage, and preserve their personal collection of poems, with flexible cloud synchronization (Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive) or in Local Only mode. The app supports multiple independent collections, each with its own cover and dedicated files.

The app offers a free version and a Premium version available through a one-time in-app purchase via Google Play. This policy describes what data is processed, for what purposes, and what rights you have as a user.

The developer does not collect or store the content of your poems on their own servers. Your collection remains on your device and in the storage space of your choice (Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, or Local Only).

Data Controller

The data controller for personal data is:

  • Edoardo Stoffa — Independent Developer
  • Email: support@poesieapp.it

For any privacy-related requests, you can contact the controller at the email address provided.

Processed Data

The app processes the following data exclusively for the functioning of the service:

  • Email address of the Google or Microsoft account (displayed in the settings screen after connecting to the respective cloud service)
  • The app handles collections, revisions, automatic drafts, trash, tags, favourites, covers, statistics and preferences. If you connect a cloud account, it handles your account profile and access tokens, as well as the local data needed for app lock and Premium verification.
  • OAuth 2.0 / PKCE authentication token for Google Drive and/or Microsoft OneDrive (saved locally using secure operating system mechanisms such as SecureStorage)
  • App preferences (e.g., selected language, visual theme, Premium activation status, collection list)
  • Custom cover image selected by the user via system picker (saved in the app's private directory)
  • Lock password: the app only saves a hash (not the plain text password) in a protected area of the device
  • Recovery code saved in a protected area of the device to allow password resets in case of loss
  • Premium purchase token (Google Play purchase token) — saved in a protected area of the device to verify purchase validity with Google Play Billing; it is never shared with the developer
  • Export and restore password-protected ZIP backups

No data is stored on servers owned by the developer.

Dictation and read-aloud use the service configured on the device, which may process audio or text on its servers under its own settings and notices. The app may locally process images, videos, custom audio, revisions and statistics. Exports and backups may be handed to the app or destination you select through the system; those copies follow the recipient’s rules. Aggregate Premium counters stay on the device and are not automatically sent to the developer.

Purposes and Legal Basis

Data is processed for:

  • Syncing and saving collections on Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive, or secure local storage on device (Local Only mode)
  • Security functions (app lock via PIN or biometrics, recovery code, local file encryption, security overlay)
  • User-requested features (cover image, language preferences, visual theme, tags, favorites, streak widget, writing goals, local backup, anthology PDF generation, local processing of statistics and "Poetry Wrapped")
  • Local notifications (writing streak reminders, restored upon device reboot)
  • In-app purchase management (verification and restoration of the Premium purchase via Google Play Billing)
Processing needed for the service you request is based on GDPR Article 6(1)(b); legal obligations on Article 6(1)(c). Device and cloud permissions are requested separately for optional features and can be revoked in their settings. Acknowledging this notice is not blanket consent to further processing.

Cloud and Local Storage (Google Drive, OneDrive, and Local Only)

Users can freely choose where to store and synchronize their collections among three modes:

  • Google Drive: The app uses Google Drive APIs with the drive.file scope, restricting access strictly to files created by the app itself. The app cannot view, read, modify, or delete any other files or folders in your Drive. You can revoke permissions at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Microsoft OneDrive: The app uses Microsoft Graph APIs with the Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder scope. Access is strictly limited to the application's dedicated folder (OneDrive > Apps > Poesie App). The app has no access to any other personal files, folders, or documents in your OneDrive account. You can manage or revoke access in your Microsoft account settings at account.live.com/consent/Manage.
  • Local Only does not sync collections to a cloud provider. Writing and organisation work on the device. Purchases, voice services and sharing you choose may still use networks and external services. Before uninstalling, save confirmed poems through an export or external backup: drafts and trash are not included.

All collection files remain your exclusive property and under your complete control, whichever mode is chosen.

Local Storage and Security

The following data is saved locally in the app's private folder, protected by the operating system and inaccessible to other applications:

  • OAuth 2.0 Google / Microsoft tokens (stored in secure operating system storage SecureStorage)
  • Main collections have AES-encrypted copies; reading, editing and export also create plaintext working files. Automatic snapshots in the private Backups folder are unencrypted DOCX files: the code retains up to 10 copies per collection-name prefix. App lock and screenshot protection do not automatically encrypt snapshots, cloud documents or exported files.
  • Preferences (e.g., language, visual theme, Premium status, list of collections, and related settings)
  • Custom cover images (only files chosen by the user via system picker)
  • Password hash + recovery code in a protected area (SecureStorage)
  • Premium purchase token in a protected area, used exclusively for local checks with Google Play
  • Local backup file (ZIP archive encrypted with a user-provided password), generated only upon explicit request and kept in temporary cache

This data is automatically deleted upon uninstalling the app. You can also remove the cloud session (Google Drive or OneDrive) at any time via "Disconnect" in settings.

Google Drive / Microsoft OneDrive: HTTPS (TLS).

Drafts and trash are encrypted and local: they are not synced or included in ZIP backups. Trashed poems can be recovered within 30 days; expired copies are removed during the next cleanup. Collections have encrypted copies, but plaintext working files, exports and private snapshots also exist. Google/Microsoft handle cloud services; Google Play handles purchases. Dictation and read-aloud use device services, which may process audio or text online. Share files only with services you trust.

Permissions Required

To function properly, the app requests minimal permissions:

No permission is used for purposes other than those specified.

Premium Version and In-App Purchases

Poesie offers a single, permanent in-app purchase (one-time purchase) via Google Play Billing.

Activating the Premium version unlocks extra features (e.g., additional visual themes, advanced editor tools, advanced export features). These features are managed entirely locally on your device, without needing a separate account.

In relation to the Premium purchase, the app processes the following data:

  • Purchase token — an opaque identifier generated by Google Play, saved in a protected area of the device (SecureStorage). It is used strictly to verify purchase validity with Google Play Billing APIs and is never sent to the developer or third parties other than Google LLC.
  • Premium status — a boolean flag saved in app preferences to remember the activation state between sessions, with periodic reconciliation with Google Play on app launch.

The developer does not directly manage payments. All financial transactions occur strictly through the Google Play Store, under its policies. See the Google Privacy Policy for payment data details.

You can restore a previous purchase (e.g., after reinstalling or changing devices) using the "Restore purchase" feature in the app, which queries Google Play without requiring a new payment.

Third-Party Sharing

Data is sent to providers needed for features you choose (Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, Google Play, voice services and sharing apps). We do not sell your data; the app contains no advertising or remote analytics services. OneDrive uses Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder for files, User.Read for your profile and offline_access for session renewal. Cloud copies are DOCX documents and do not use the local app encryption key: Poesie does not provide end-to-end encryption for them.

Third-party services used for technical operations:

  • Google LLC — OAuth 2.0 and Google Drive storage. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google LLC / Google Play — In-app purchase processing via Google Play Billing and in-app review prompts. The purchase token is sent to Google solely to validate the Premium purchase; the developer has no access to payment details. Google Play Terms
  • Microsoft OneDrive — Privacy

Dictation and read-aloud use the service configured on the device, which may process audio or text on its servers under its own settings and notices. The app may locally process images, videos, custom audio, revisions and statistics. Exports and backups may be handed to the app or destination you select through the system; those copies follow the recipient’s rules. Aggregate Premium counters stay on the device and are not automatically sent to the developer.

Data Retention

  • Drafts stay on the device until confirmed or discarded. Trash keeps encrypted copies recoverable within 30 days; expired copies are removed on the next opening/cleanup. Unreadable files may be retained to avoid losing recoverable data. Drafts and trash are not included in sync or ZIP backups. Deleting a poem or emptying the trash does not automatically remove earlier snapshots, exports, or cloud-provider versions and trash. Uninstalling removes private local data, not external copies. Disconnecting the cloud does not delete cloud files.
  • OAuth authentication tokens (Google or Microsoft) remain on the device until you disconnect or uninstall the app
  • The recovery code remains on the device until you remove the password/lock or uninstall the app
  • The Premium purchase token remains on the device until you uninstall the app; it is automatically removed if the purchase is no longer active on Google Play

User Rights (GDPR)

As a data subject, under Articles 15-22 of the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access — Obtain confirmation of data processing and a copy of your data
  • Rectification — Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure — Request deletion of your data
  • Restriction — Request restriction of processing under certain circumstances
  • Portability — Receive your data in a structured format
  • Objection — Object to processing where applicable
  • Complaint — File a complaint with the Data Protection Authority (www.garanteprivacy.it)

To exercise your rights, contact the controller at the email address provided in the "Data Controller" section.
