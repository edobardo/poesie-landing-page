03 — BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Questions, asked in quiet

Where are my poems stored?

Your choice: in your Google Drive account, on Microsoft OneDrive, or exclusively on your device in Local Only mode. You decide where your poems reside.

Can anyone else read my drafts?

Only if you choose to share them. Your journal opens only for you.

Do I need a separate account?

No, no separate or proprietary account. You can connect your Google or Microsoft account for cloud sync, or use the app without any account in Local Only mode.

What if a poem is only half-written?

Leave it as it is. It will wait for you on its page until you're ready to return.
