BEFORE YOUR FIRST VERSE

A few answers.

Can I start without an account?

Yes. Choose Local Only mode and start writing on your device. You can connect a cloud account later.

Where are my poems stored?

On your device, with optional sync to your own Google Drive or OneDrive. One storage provider is active at a time.

Can I create images and videos for free?

Yes, with the free styles and the app watermark. Premium adds more styles, watermark removal and custom audio for videos.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. It is a one-time unlock through Google Play. See prices and features in the app; use the same Google account to restore your purchase.

Which devices can I use?

Poesie is available for Android on Google Play. Check the store listing for compatibility with your device.
